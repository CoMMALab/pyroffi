## IOSP (`python -m iosp.make_figures`)

| slot | output | renderer | data | run config |
|------|--------|----------|------|------------|
| flagship example | `fig2_multistart.{pdf,png}` | `iosp.viz.fig_multistart` | `data/results/multistart/joint_seed0.npz` | `multistart_robustness.yaml` |
| cross-domain table | `table_domains.tex` | `iosp.analysis.table_domains` | `data/results/{pickplace,tetris,tower}/` | `pickplace_iosp.yaml`, `tetris_iosp.yaml`, `tower_iosp.yaml` |
| subspace theory | `fig_eigenspectrum.{pdf,png}` | `iosp.viz.fig_eigenspectrum` | `data/results/identifiability/eigen_projection.npz` | `identifiability_spectrum.yaml` |

`fig2_multistart` panels: (a) per-candidate held-out EE RMSE vs. outer step,
coloured by IK branch, winner highlighted — this is the recovery-loss curve;
(b) winner's 3D EE path vs. the demonstration on the held-out scene;
(c) per-branch best held-out RMSE.

`table_domains` columns: uniform-`theta` baseline, the IOSP fit, and the
oracle `theta*` floor, all as held-out EE RMSE (m); normalised regret
`(fit - oracle) / (baseline - oracle)`, which is 0 for perfect recovery and 1
for no better than uniform. `||Δθ||` is reported last and is **context, not
the criterion** — see `fig_eigenspectrum`.

`fig_eigenspectrum` panels: (a) eigenvalue spectrum of the feature-gradient
Gram matrix, log scale, split at the 95%-cumulative-trace rank; (b) recovery
error decomposed onto the identifiable and near-null subspaces. Panel (b) is
the reason the table's `||Δθ||` column carries a "context" label: the raw
norm is dominated by the component the demonstrations structurally cannot
observe.
